analysis: makes an argument backed by a specific, checkable fact such as a box score statistic, shooting percentage, contract number, historical record, or advanced metric
hot_take: a confident, controversial, or sweeping claim about a player, team, or narrative with no factual evidence or statistical support offered
reaction: an immediate, personal emotional response to a recent play, game result, or breaking news, capturing feelings of shock, joy, misery, or excitement rather than making an argument
